# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

**Version:** 3.2
**Last revised:** October 2024
**Department:** Human Resources

## Welcome to Acme Corp

Thank you for joining our team. This handbook provides an overview of Acme Corp's policies, benefits, and expectations for all employees. Please read it carefully and reach out to HR with any questions.

## Our Mission

Acme Corp builds tools that help businesses operate more efficiently. We are committed to innovation, customer success, and creating a workplace where talented people thrive.

## Employment Basics

### Employment Classification

- **Full-time**: 40 hours per week, eligible for all benefits
- **Part-time**: 20–39 hours per week, eligible for prorated benefits
- **Contract**: Defined engagement period, benefits per contract terms

### Work Hours

Standard business hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM**, Monday through Friday. Exempt employees may have flexible start/end times with manager approval, as long as core hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM) are covered.

### Probationary Period

All new hires undergo a 90-day probationary period. During this time, both the employee and Acme Corp may end the employment relationship with 1 week's notice. Performance reviews are conducted at the 30, 60, and 90-day marks.

## Compensation & Benefits

### Pay Schedule

Employees are paid semi-monthly (1st and 15th of each month) via direct deposit. If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is issued on the preceding business day.

### Health Insurance

Acme Corp provides comprehensive health insurance to all full-time employees and their dependents:

- **Medical**: Choice of PPO or HMO plans, employer covers 85% of premiums
- **Dental**: Included at no additional cost
- **Vision**: Included at no additional cost
- **Mental Health**: Access to Lyra Health for therapy and coaching (12 sessions/year)

Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.

### Retirement

We offer a 401(k) plan with a 4% employer match. Employees are eligible to enroll immediately upon hire, with the match vesting over 3 years.

### Professional Development

Each employee receives a $2,000 annual learning budget for conferences, courses, books, and certifications. Requests are approved by your manager.

## Time Off & Leave

### Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive **15 days** of paid time off per year, accrued on a per-pay-period basis. Unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days.

| Tenure | Annual PTO |
|--------|-----------|
| 0–3 years | 15 days |
| 4–6 years | 20 days |
| 7+ years | 25 days |

PTO requests must be submitted through our HR platform with appropriate advance notice (5 business days for 3+ day requests).

### Sick Leave

Employees receive 8 sick days per year. Extended illness beyond 5 consecutive days requires medical documentation.

### Parental Leave

- Primary caregiver: 12 weeks paid
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid

## Workplace Policies

### Office Attendance

Acme Corp values in-person collaboration. Our standard work arrangement is **office-first with limited remote flexibility**. Employees may work from home up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.

- Tuesday and Thursday are company-wide in-office days
- Additional in-office days may be required during project milestones
- Remote work arrangements are subject to quarterly review

### Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Maintain confidentiality of company and customer information
- Report conflicts of interest to HR
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations
- Use company resources responsibly

### Communication

- **Email**: Primary channel for formal communications
- **Slack**: Real-time collaboration and quick questions
- **Meetings**: Default to 30 minutes; include an agenda
- **Documentation**: Important decisions must be documented in writing

### Dress Code

Business casual is the standard for office days. Client-facing meetings may require business professional attire. Use good judgment for remote meetings.

## Technology & Security

### Equipment

All full-time employees receive:

- MacBook Pro (or equivalent, based on role)
- External monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Noise-canceling headphones (engineering roles)

Equipment must be returned upon termination.

### Information Security

- Use strong, unique passwords for all company accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication on all services
- Do not share credentials or access tokens
- Lock your laptop when stepping away
- Report suspected security incidents to #security immediately

### Acceptable Use

Company equipment and accounts are for business use. Incidental personal use is acceptable as long as it doesn't interfere with work, violate policies, or create security risks.

## Performance & Growth

### Performance Reviews

Formal reviews are conducted twice per year (June and December). Reviews include:

- Self-assessment
- Manager assessment
- Peer feedback (optional but encouraged)
- Goal setting for the next period

### Promotions

Promotions are based on sustained performance, scope expansion, and business need. The promotion cycle runs annually in Q1, with off-cycle promotions considered for exceptional cases.

## Separation

### Resignation

Employees are asked to provide at least 2 weeks' notice. Please submit your resignation in writing to your manager and HR.

### Offboarding

Upon departure:

1. Return all company equipment
2. Transfer ownership of projects and documents
3. Complete an exit interview with HR
4. Access to company systems is revoked on the last day
