# Time Off & Remote Work Policy

**Effective:** January 1, 2025
**Department:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All full-time employees globally

## Philosophy

At Acme Corp, we believe that well-rested teams build better products. We encourage everyone to take their full vacation allocation each year and to work in the environment where they are most productive.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Annual Allocation

All full-time employees receive **20 days** of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. This is in addition to public holidays observed in your office location.

| Tenure | PTO Days | Accrual Rate |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| 0–2 years | 20 days | 1.67 days/month |
| 3–5 years | 25 days | 2.08 days/month |
| 6+ years | 30 days | 2.50 days/month |

### Requesting Time Off

- Submit PTO requests through BambooHR at least **2 weeks in advance** for requests of 3+ days
- Shorter absences (1–2 days) require at least 3 business days' notice
- Managers should respond to PTO requests within 2 business days
- Requests during peak periods (product launches, quarter-end) may require team coordination

### PTO Guidelines

- You may carry over up to 5 unused days to the next calendar year
- Carried-over days expire on March 31 of the following year
- PTO cannot be exchanged for cash except upon termination
- We encourage taking at least one block of 5+ consecutive days per year

### Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year for personal illness or caring for an immediate family member. Absences longer than 3 consecutive days may require a doctor's note.

## Remote Work Policy

### Hybrid Schedule

Acme Corp operates on a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, with the expectation that they are present in the office on designated team days.

- Each team sets its own in-office days (typically 2 fixed days per week)
- Your manager will communicate your team's schedule during onboarding
- In-office days are coordinated to maximize collaboration and face time

### Remote Work Guidelines

When working remotely, employees are expected to:

- Be available during core hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM local time)
- Keep their calendar up to date with their working location
- Join meetings on video with camera on when possible
- Ensure a reliable internet connection suitable for video calls

### Requesting Fully Remote Periods

Occasionally, employees may need to work fully remote for an extended period (e.g., family obligations, relocation transition). Requests for more than 2 consecutive weeks of remote work should be submitted to your manager and People Ops for approval.

### International Remote Work

Working from a country other than your employment contract's jurisdiction requires prior approval from People Ops due to tax and legal implications. Please submit requests at least 30 days in advance.

## Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks fully paid
- **Secondary caregiver**: 6 weeks fully paid
- May be taken any time within the first 12 months after birth or adoption
- Discuss your leave plan with your manager and People Ops at least 8 weeks before the expected start date

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member, and up to 3 days for extended family.
