# HR Policies

**Version:** 2.0
**Last updated:** 2023-11-15
**Owner:** People Operations

---

## PTO Policy

Employees receive **15 days of paid time off annually**. For employees with
more than 3 years of tenure, this increases to 20 days. Contractors are
not eligible for PTO benefits.

Submit all PTO requests through Gusto with at least 5 business days notice.

---

## Remote Work Policy

Remote work requires manager approval and is limited to **2 days per month**
for roles that are primarily office-based. Fully remote roles are defined in
the position description.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a suitable home
office environment.

---

## Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

- Meals: up to $25 per person without approval
- Office supplies: up to $100 without approval
- Software subscriptions: require IT approval regardless of cost
- Anything over **$25** requires manager approval via the expense portal

---

## Onboarding Checklist

All new hires must complete the following steps in their first two weeks:

1. Complete I-9 and tax forms via Gusto
2. Set up laptop using the IT setup guide
3. Join #general, #engineering, and #people-ops on Slack
4. Schedule a 1:1 with your manager within the first 3 days
5. Complete security training (mandatory, due within 7 days)
6. Review the Employee Handbook and sign acknowledgment
7. Complete benefits enrollment within 30 days of start date

---

## Code of Conduct

Acme expects all employees to act with integrity, respect colleagues, and
protect confidential information. Violations should be reported to HR or
via the anonymous ethics hotline.

Detailed policies on harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are in
the separate Code of Conduct document.

---

## Benefits

- Health insurance: Acme covers 90% of premium for employee, 50% for dependents
- 401(k): 4% match, vesting over 3 years
- Learning & development: $1,000 annual budget per employee
- Parental leave: 12 weeks primary / 6 weeks secondary caregiver

Benefits questions: benefits@acme.com
