## Welcome

Welcome to Acme! This handbook covers the policies, benefits, and norms you
need to know as a full-time employee.

---

## Time Off

### PTO

You get **20 days of paid time off per year**. PTO accrues monthly at 1.67 days
per month. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 10 days.

To request PTO, submit a request in Gusto at least 3 business days in advance
for up to 5 consecutive days, and 2 weeks in advance for longer periods.

### Sick Leave

Sick days are separate from PTO. Take as needed — just notify your manager by
9 AM if you won't be in.

### Holidays

Acme observes 10 federal holidays plus a flexible "culture day" you can use
on any date of your choosing.

---

## Remote Work

Acme is a **remote-first company**. All roles are eligible for fully remote work
unless otherwise stated in your offer letter.

For team meetings, we use GMT+1 as a reference timezone for scheduling. Core
collaboration hours are 10 AM – 2 PM GMT+1.

### Home Office Stipend

New hires receive a one-time $1,500 home office stipend in their first
paycheck. Recurring equipment needs go through the standard purchasing process.

---

## Expenses

Expenses under $50 can be submitted without prior approval.
Team meals up to $30 per person are covered for official team events.
Anything over $50 needs your manager's approval via Ramp before you spend.

---

We run semi-annual performance reviews — one in Q1 and one in Q3.

## Engineering Career Ladder

Acme uses a 6-level engineering career ladder (E1–E6). Promotions require
documented evidence of sustained performance at the next level and are
approved by your skip-level manager.

See the Engineering Handbook for the full rubric.
